1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies solely to the Android mobile application UUD 1945 Handbook, application ID com.uud.uud1945referensi, developed and published on Google Play by Adjie Santoso. It covers no other application or service.

2. Disclaimer regarding government affiliation

This application is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representative of any government entity.

  • Not a government app: We do not represent the Government of the Republic of Indonesia or any other government body.
  • Source of information: The legal texts in the app are compiled from publicly available official government sources: JDIH (jdihn.go.id) and Peraturan.go.id. The app is a tool for accessing public information.

3. Information the app collects

3.1. Data stored only on your device

Your bookmarks and their notes, quiz best scores and daily streak, chosen theme, chosen language, the saved "Article of the Day", and whether you have seen the one-time feature tour are stored locally on your device using Android's app preference storage. This data is never transmitted to us, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

3.2. Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used. It records screen views and in-app interactions (such as opening a document, tapping the quiz card, or a banner ad being shown), together with standard technical information: device model, operating system version, app version, coarse country or region derived from your IP address, and a randomly generated app instance identifier. This information is not used to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with any personal data.

3.3. Anonymous account

The app signs you in to Firebase Authentication with an anonymous account. This creates a randomly generated account identifier held by Google on our behalf. It contains no name, email address, phone number, or password, and we do not ask you for any of these. It exists only to keep app state consistent, and it is not used to identify you as a person.

3.4. Advertising

The app displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may access your device's Advertising ID and other technical identifiers, and may use them for ad personalisation and frequency control. This processing is performed by Google under its own privacy policy.

3.5. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, precise location, or the contents of what you read, search for, or write in your bookmark notes.

4. Offline access

All legal texts are bundled inside the app. Reading and searching them requires no internet connection, and the app does not download legal content from any remote server. An internet connection is used only for the advertising and analytics described above.

5. Notifications

The app can show a daily "Article of the Day" notification. It is generated on your device from the bundled legal texts and scheduled locally. No notification content is sent from, or transmitted to, any server. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Android settings.

6. Permissions

The app declares the following Android permissions, some of which are added automatically by the Google SDKs it uses:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — required to load advertisements and send analytics.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — required to show the daily "Article of the Day" notification.
  • AD_ID / ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS — added by the Google AdMob SDK to serve and measure advertising.
  • WAKE_LOCK, VIBRATE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, READ_GSERVICES, BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE — added by the Google Firebase and AdMob SDKs for scheduled notifications and standard Google Play services functionality.

The app requests no permission for your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or precise location.

7. Third-party services

The app uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Authentication — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Privacy Policy — policies.google.com/privacy

8. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties other than the Google services listed in section 7, which process it as our service providers in order to operate advertising and analytics.

9. Retention and deletion

Data held on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time through Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device, and you can opt out of ad personalisation there. To request deletion of the anonymous account identifier described in section 3.3, email us at the address below and we will delete it.

10. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can delete it.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any change will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Please review the page periodically.
