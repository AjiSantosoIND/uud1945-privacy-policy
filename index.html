3. Information We Collect

3.1. Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location) through the UUD 1945 Handbook application. You can use the app without providing any personal details.

3.2. Usage Data

We do not use third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics or Firebase) to track your usage of the app. Your usage history and search queries are stored locally on your device (if applicable) and are not shared with us.

3.3. Advertising

This app integrates Google AdMob to display banner ads. Ads are currently disabled while our AdMob account is under review. Once enabled, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other technical identifiers to serve and measure ads. This data is processed by Google in accordance with their own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Offline Access and Third-Party Services

All legal texts are bundled with the app itself, so no internet connection is required to browse or read them. The app does not fetch content from any remote server. Google AdMob is integrated to serve advertising within the app (currently disabled, see 3.3); when active, it requires an internet connection.

5. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions on your device:

  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): Added automatically by the Google AdMob SDK. Ads are currently disabled, so this identifier is not yet used; it will be used by AdMob to serve and measure ads once ads are enabled.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
